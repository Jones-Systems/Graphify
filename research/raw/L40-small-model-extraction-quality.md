## Verdict
Top pick: **Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507** (Apache-2.0, ≈2.4 GiB Q4_K_M, best IFEval/BFCL of the four) behind **llama.cpp server + JSON-schema constrained decoding**, invoked as a **GLiNER-gated nightly** relation/triple extractor — expect ~8–12 s/doc single-stream (~2–2.5× aggregate with `-np 4`), entities stay in GLiNER/NuNER.
For closed-schema RE quality-per-ms, pilot **ReLiK** first: encoder F1 94–95 (NYT) at seconds-per-testset makes every generative option look slow and inaccurate on CPU; **NuExtract3** (Apache-2.0, Qwen3.5-4B base) is the current-gen upgrade path if schema-field extraction dominates.
Llama-3.2-3B only if RAM-bound; Qwen2.5-7B only if 4B quality proves insufficient (~2× cost, ~5.5 GiB); Phi-4-mini's edge is its MIT license.
