VERDICT: The recorded candidate is a systemd OnCalendar timer with Persistent=true plus a manual trigger of the same oneshot `graph-index-rebuild.service`. A watcher may set a debounced dirty hint when lower latency is later required, but callbacks should not mutate the index and any overflow must force a rescan. Skip plain cron unless catch-up is separately guaranteed, and do not use watchdog as the sole correctness mechanism.
