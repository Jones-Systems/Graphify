VERDICT: Top pick = cron-driven via a systemd OnCalendar timer with Persistent=true plus manual `systemctl start` of the same oneshot service — the nightly+manual SLA gains nothing from push latency, while file-watch carries silent-event-loss risk (watchdog literally discards IN_Q_OVERFLOW; kernel drops events past 16384 queued) and a resident-process cost. Integration: `codex-index-rebuild.service` (Type=oneshot, Nice=10, IOSchedulingClass=idle) + `.timer` (OnCalendar=*-*-* 02:30, Persistent=true, RandomizedDelaySec=15m); only if sub-hour freshness later becomes a requirement, bolt on a watchfiles(1600/50) dirty-marker writing a staging-hint file that the rebuild consumes — never indexing in-callback, always reconciled by the nightly full pass. Skip anacron (absent on this box; plain cron would skip missed nights silently) and skip watchdog entirely.
