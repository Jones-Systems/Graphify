## Verdict (≤3 lines)

**Top pick:** bge-reranker-v2-m3 (568M, Apache-2.0) as an ONNX-int8 post-fusion stage via fastembed TextCrossEncoder (custom registration; FlagEmbedding fallback): rerank gated-3-way-RRF top-50→top-10 in ≲1.5 s on 16 cores, ~0.54 BEIR nDCG@10, ~0.5–1 GB RAM (far under the 3 GiB floor); optionally add Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B with yes/no-logit scoring as an offline/nightly deep-rerank tier only.

**Honest CPU verdict on generative 3–8B reranking:** reject for interactive paths — 8B pointwise is prefill-bound (~2–3.5 min/query at 16-core rates), listwise (RankZephyr/RankGPT-style) is worse, and position bias is severe (nDCG 65.8→25.2 under input shuffle; instability documented into 2026) while every known fix (permutation self-consistency ×5–20, DebiasFirst retraining, adaptive repetition) multiplies cost or requires training; verbosity bias additionally makes LLM scores gameable by long stuffed documents.

**Does constrained output help?** Only as two-token logit scoring (P(yes) from final-token logits — no parse drift, prefill-only batching, proven in production by Qwen3-Reranker); JSON-grammar constraints guarantee syntax but measurably degrade reasoning and do nothing about position/verbosity bias.

### Integration sketch for our stack
`gated 3-way RRF (top-50)` → `fastembed TextCrossEncoder(custom: onnx-community/bge-reranker-v2-m3-ONNX, threads≤4, max_len 256–512)` → sigmoid-normalize → splice into final ranking (weighted splice or hard top-k replacement). tantivy/sqlite-vec/graphifyy untouched; Splink/GLiNER untouched. Pin model hash + fastembed==0.8.x; smoke-test MemAvailable stays ≥3 GiB (expected headroom >2 GiB). If an LLM reranker is ever adopted despite this verdict: Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B Q4 via llama.cpp, top-20 candidates, logit diff scoring, offline only.
