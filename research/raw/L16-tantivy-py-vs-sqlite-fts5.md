Verdict: Top pick — adopt **tantivy-py 0.26.0** (MIT, cp313 manylinux wheel verified 2026-04-29, in-process, zero services) as the BM25 layer for all 18 repos; it beats FTS5 where it matters for hybrid search (multi-field BM25F-style weighting, phrase/fuzzy/prefix query grammar, snippets, aggregations, Python-custom tokenizers, bounded-writer mmap readers) while FTS5 stays as the zero-cost fallback already in stdlib. At our scale both fit easily, but tantivy's measured sub-ms P50 at 6M docs, >27k docs/s builds, and explicit heap_size budget respect the 3GiB floor. Integration: nightly rebuild per repo of a chunk-level index (chunk_id,repo,path,symbol_path,body; writer heap_size≈512MB, threads≤8), then RRF-fuse BM25 ranks with graphifyy PPR ranks (and LanceDB vectors when phase 2 lands).
